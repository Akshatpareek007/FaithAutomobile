# Faith Automation Works

> Turnkey Automotive Assembly Lines, 6-Axis Robotic Welding Cells, Vision Inspection & Precision Tool Room

Official web platform for **Faith Automation Works**, Gurgaon (Established June 2015). Direct manufacturer of Special Purpose Machines (SPMs), checking fixtures, and automated production systems for Tier-1 automotive manufacturers across India (Hanon, Lumax, JRG, Hero Motors, BorgWarner, PPAP, and more).

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Pages

- **Multi-Page Architecture**:
  - `index.html` — Homepage with interactive hero, live KPI metrics bar, and capability highlights.
  - `capabilities.html` — Deep-dive into 9 core engineering domains & 5-stage turnkey execution methodology.
  - `machines.html` — Catalog of 43+ field-tested machines with category filtering and technical modal inspection.
  - `toolroom.html` — In-house machining infrastructure specs (Manford VMC, M1TR with DRO, Grinders, Lathes) and workforce breakdown.
  - `why-us.html` — Leadership profile of Managing Director Mr. Kaushal K Khandelwal and official Quality Policy.
  - `clients.html` — Directory of 30+ Tier-1 automotive and industrial clients with case project deliveries.
  - `rfq.html` — Real-time interactive RFQ feasibility estimator and engineering consultation form.
- **Design System**: Bespoke industrial cyber-mechanics theme with deep obsidian backgrounds, Faith crimson accents (`#E11D48`), high-tech automation cyan (`#0EA5E9`), and glassmorphism.
- **Real Imagery**: Extracted and optimized authentic equipment photography from plant installations.
